# Visual direction

The owner will supply a separate reference image in Google AI Studio. Use it as a composition reference after it arrives. Create original MAZ IMPEX layouts and use approved MAZ IMPEX copy and assets.

- Open with a cinematic dark photographic hero, transparent dark header, bold white heading and restrained metallic-gold quotation actions.
- Follow with white and off-white sections, a dark manufacturing capability strip and four capability cards that become a readable stack on phones.
- Use premium photographic product sections and a clear route from category browsing to product information, customisation and enquiries.
- Use MAZ IMPEX subjects such as boxing gloves, MMA and Muay Thai equipment, shin guards, martial arts uniforms, team kits, sportswear and custom apparel.
- Build hierarchy through type, spacing and strong photography. Keep gradients limited, animation lightweight, backgrounds calm, and actions aligned to OEM/ODM buyers.
- Preserve the approved logo and keep its clear space. Use the exported neutral logo slot as a spacing reference during public review.

Place real manufacturing claims only where they already exist or the owner supplies supporting copy. [Page structure references](reference-previews/README.md)
